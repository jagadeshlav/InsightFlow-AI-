1. INTRODUCTION: GREAT PIRATE ERA START & GOL D. ROGER LEGEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mawa, One Piece anedi simple ga oka anime or manga mathrame kaadu... idi oka modern mythology, oka legendary master-piece! Eiichiro Oda create chesina ee grand world lo freedom, dreams, friendship (nakama), politics, racism, ancient mysteries, and unshakeable will power untayi.

Katha start ayyedi oka legendary pirate tho â€” athane "Pirate King" Gol D. Roger!
Roger tanadi antha sampadinchadu: Wealth, Fame, Power... prapancham lo unna anni samudhralani daati, Grand Line chivara unna mysterious island "Laugh Tale" ni reach ayina oke okka vyakthi Roger. Kani Roger ki oka incurable disease untundi. Tana chivari rojullo, World Government ki surrender avthadu.

Loguetown lo Roger execution roju... antha silent ga undi. Scaffold meeda unna Roger ni oka random person aduguthadu:
"Oye Pirate King! Nuvvu sampadinchina aasthulantha ekkada dachaav? Grand Line lo ekkado unda?"

Roger oka pedda chirunavvu navvuthu, prapanchaniki vinipinchela chepthaadu:
"Naa treasure kavala? Teeskolandi! Prapancham lo unna sampada antha oka chota dachesanu... velli vethukondi!"

Aa okka maata tho "Great Pirate Era" (Dai Kaizoku Jidai) modhalayindi! Lakshala mandi manushulu, tana tana dreams tho pirate flags ethi, Grand Line vaipu samudhralaloki dookesaru. Kani 20 samvatsaralu gadichina, evvaru aa One Piece ni kanugonalekapoyaru.

Ilaanti time lo, East Blue ane oka prashanthamaina ocean lo oka chinna pillaadu tana journey start chesthaadu... athane mana Monkey D. Luffy!

2. EAST BLUE SAGA: THE ADVENTURE BEGINS (ROMANCE DAWN TO LOGUETOWN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ROMANCE DAWN & SHANKS PROMISE]
Luffy Windmill Village (Foosha Village) lo peruguthadu. Akkada Red-Haired Shanks ane oka cool pirate crew tana base ga untundi. Luffy ki pirate avvalani pichi korika. Shanks ni tana crew lo teeskomani roju gola chesthaadu. Tana guts chupinchadaniki tana kanti kinda knife tho oka cut kuda pettukuntadu (ade tana iconic scar).

Oka roju pub lo, Shanks teskochhina oka mysterious treasure box lo unna fruit ni Luffy theliyaka thinesthadu. Ade "Gomu Gomu no Mi" (Devil Fruit - Paramecia/Hito Hito no Mi Model: Nika). Adi tinnaka Luffy body rubber laaga saaguthundi, kani saapam enti ante bathikinantha kaalam ocean lo eetharaadu, water lo padithe rayila munigipothadu.

Oka local mountain bandit Higuma vachi Shanks ni avamanisthadu, beer posi insult chesthaadu. Shanks mathram navvesi lite theeskuntaadu. Idi chusi Luffy kopam tho bandit meedhaki velthadu. Bandit Luffy ni kidnap chesi sea loki theskelli padesthadu. Akkada Sea King (samudhra rakshasi) vachi Luffy ni mingadaniki vasthundi. Right in the nick of time, Shanks vachi Luffy ni kapadathadu... kani aa process lo Shanks tana left cheyini pogottukuntadu!

Luffy edusthunte Shanks antadu: "Chinna cheyyi matrame kada Luffy, nuvvu safe ga unnav, anthe chalu."
Shanks ooru vadili velle time lo, tana favorite Straw Hat (gaddi topi) ni Luffy thalapai petti antadu:
"Luffy, ee topi naaku chaala precious. Nuvvu oka goppa pirate ayyi, nannu daati poyinappudu... ee topi ni naaku thirigi ivvu!"
Aa roju nunchi Luffy target okkate: "Nenu Pirate King avthanu!"

10 samvatsarala tarvatha, 17 years vachaka Luffy oka chinna chekka barrel/boat lo tana solo pirate journey start chesthadu.


[ZORO RECRUITMENT - SHELLS TOWN]
Luffy ocean meeda velli Marine base unna Shells Town ki vasthadu. Akkada "Pirate Hunter" Roronoa Zoro ane deadly swordsman ni corrupt Marine Captain Axe-Hand Morgan bandhinchi unchuthadu. Zoro oka chinna ammayi kosam tana meeda unna punishment ni bharisthuntadu.
Luffy Zoro daggiriki velli: "Nee swords nenu theskostha, nuvvu naa crew lo cheru!" antadu.
Zoro first reject chesina, Luffy tanani save chesi tana 3 swords ichesariki oppukuntadu. Zoro dream enti ante: "World's Greatest Swordsman" avvadam (tana childhood friend Kuina ki ichina promise). Luffy & Zoro pirate duo form avthundi!


[ORANGE TOWN & SYRUP VILLAGE]
Next, Orange Town lo Luffy Buggy the Clown (Chop Chop Fruit user) ni encounter chesthadu. Akkada Nami ane clever thief ni kalusthaaru. Nami pirates ni asahyam cheskuntundi kani treasure kosam Luffy tho temporary alliance pettukuntundhi.

Tarvatha Syrup Village ki veltharu. Akkada Usopp ane oka pedda abadhala koru (liar), kani manchi manasuna sniper untadu. Kuro ane oka black-hearted pirate tana rich friend Kaya ni champi tana property kotteyadaniki plan chesthadu. Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Usopp kalisi Kuro Pirates ni odistharu.
Happy ga Kaya vellaki "Going Merry" ane tana ship ni gift ga isthundi! Usopp kuda Straw Hat Pirates lo Sniper ga official ga join avthadu.


[BARATIE & MIHAWK ENCOUNTER]
Samudhrameeda floating restaurant "Baratie" ki Straw Hats reach avtharu. Akkada Sanji ane oka bad-ass cook untadu, athani feet tho mathrame fight chesthadu (hands are only for cooking ani athani rule). Sanji dream: "All Blue" (prapancham lo unna anni ocean fishes oka chota unde swargam) kanugonadam.

Ade time lo, Grand Line nunchi Don Krieg ane pirate armada vasthundi. Kani valla 50 ships ni single hand tho narakadam chesina World's Greatest Swordsman "Dracule Mihawk" akkada appear avthadu.
Zoro Mihawk tho duel aduguthadu. Mihawk oka chinna pocket knife tho Zoro 3-sword attacks ni aapesthadu. Mihawk greatness chusi Zoro tana chathi ni open chesi: "Scars on the back are a swordsman's shame" ani cut theeskuntadu. Mihawk impress ayyi Zoro ni champakunda vadilesi: "Nannu daati ra, strong avvu Zoro!" antadu.
Zoro tana kanti nunchi tears tho Luffy ki promise chesthaadu: "Luffy! I will NEVER lose again! Pirate King daggira Greatest Swordsman undali kada!"
Luffy Don Krieg ni smash chesaka, Sanji tana father-figure Chef Zeff ki emotional bye cheppi cook ga join avthadu.


[ARLONG PARK - EMOTIONAL PEAK]
Nami Going Merry ni theesukoni tana home island Cocoyasi Village ki paaripothundi. Straw Hats tana venta veltharu.
Akkada truth bayatapaduthundi: Arlong ane Fish-Man pirate Nami village ni occupy chesi, tana foster mother Bell-mere ni Nami kalla munde champesadu. Nami tana village ni 100 million Berries petti konadaniki Arlong daggira navigator ga, map maker ga banya banisa ga panichesthondi. Kani Arlong corrupt Marines tho kalisi Nami dachina 100 million Berries ni dobbesthaadu.

Despair lo Nami tana chethi meeda unna Arlong tattoo ni knife tho poduchukuntu edusthundi. Luffy akkada silent ga nilabadi untadu.
Nami venakki thirigi tears tho antundi: "Luffy... help me..."
Luffy ventane tana precious Straw Hat ni Nami thalapai petti, aakasam vaipu chusi arusthadu:
"OF COURSE I WILL!!!" (ATARIMAE DAAA!)

Straw Hats 4 members (Luffy, Zoro, Sanji, Usopp) Arlong Park vaipu walk chestharu (Legendary anime walk). Luffy Arlong Park ni baddhalu kotti, Nami ni banisa chesina room ni pikkikosi, Arlong ni Gomu Gomu no Battle Axe tho ground floor varaku thokkesthadu!
Nami officially Straw Hat Pirates ki Navigator ga join avthundi. Luffy meeda 30 Million Berries first bounty paduthundi!


[LOGUETOWN: THE TOWN OF BEGINNING AND END]
Grand Line loki enter ayye mundu chivari stop Loguetown (Gol D. Roger puttina & chanipoyina place).
Buggy & Alvida Luffy ni Roger execute ayina scaffold meedhaki theskelli execute cheyadaniki try chestharu. Lightning vachi Luffy ni kapaduthundi.
Marine Captain Smoker (Smoke Fruit user) Luffy ni arrest cheyabothe, oka mysterious hooded man Smoker ni aapesthadu... athane "Monkey D. Dragon" (World's Worst Criminal, Revolutionary Army Leader, and Luffy's real father!).
Straw Hats Grand Line entrance vaipu bayalderutharu. Oka barrel meeda prathi okkaru tana dream chepthaaru:
- Sanji: "To find All Blue!"
- Luffy: "To become the Pirate King!"
- Zoro: "To become the Greatest Swordsman!"
- Nami: "To draw a map of the entire world!"
- Usopp: "To become a brave warrior of the sea!"

3. ARABASTA SAGA: THE DESERT KINGDOM & CROCODILE CONSPIRACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[REVERSE MOUNTAIN & WHISKEY PEAK]
Straw Hats Reverse Mountain ane vertical water current meeda ekkuthu Grand Line loki enter avtharu. Akkada "Laboon" ane oka giant whale ni kalusthaaru. Laboon 50 years nunchi tana crew kosam wait chesthundi. Luffy Laboon tho fight chesi, tana forehead meeda Straw Hat logo vesi: "Nenu Grand Line mottham thirigi vachi neetho malli fight chestha, appativaraku ee logo chepiypokunda chusko!" ani promise chesthadu.

Next Whiskey Peak lo Baroque Works ane secret criminal syndicate Straw Hats ni target chesthundi. Akkada Princess Nefertari Vivi (Arabasta rajyam rajakuamri) disguise lo untundi. Tana rajyanni kapadadaniki Baroque Works loki infiltrate ayyindi ani telusthundi. Baroque Works leader evaro kaadu: Warlord of the Sea "Sir Crocodile"! Straw Hats Vivi ki help cheyadaniki decide avtharu.


[LITTLE GARDEN & DRUM ISLAND]
Little Garden ane prehistoric island lo Dorry & Brogy ane 100 years ga fight cheskuntunna giant warriors ni kalustharu.
Tarvatha Nami ki high fever vasthundi. Doctor kosam Drum Island ki veltharu.
Drum Island lo corrupt King Wapol tana prajalani vadilesi paaripoyuntadu. Akkada Dr. Kureha and oka blue-nosed reindeer "Tony Tony Chopper" untadu. Chopper Human-Human Fruit (Hito Hito no Mi) tinna reindeer doctor. Tana mentor Dr. Hiluluk cherry blossoms tho manushula manasulani cure cheyachu ani nammi chanipothadu.
Hiluluk iconic quote: "Manishi eppudu chanipothadu telusa? Bullet gundello diginapuda? Kaadu! Incurable disease vachinapuda? Kaadu! Poison soup taginapuda? Kaadu! Manishi prapancham tanni marchipoyinappude chanipothadu!"
Luffy Wapol ni aakasam loki thannesi, Chopper ni tana crew lo Doctor ga recruit chesthaadu. Dr. Kureha island motham pink cherry blossom snow tho cover chesi Chopper ki send-off isthundi.


[ARABASTA KINGDOM & WARLORD CROCODILE]
Arabasta desert kingdom lo Crocodile "Dance Powder" use chesi rain padakunda chesi, King Cobra meeda bad name theppinchi civil war create chesthadu. Crocodile main goal: Arabasta lo dagi unna Ancient Weapon "Pluton" info unna Poneglyph ni kanugonadam. Crocodile partner "Nico Robin" (Miss All Sunday), prapancham lo unna oke okka Poneglyph read cheyagala person.

Luffy brother "Portgas D. Ace" (Whitebeard Pirates 2nd Division Commander, Flame-Flame Fruit user) Arabasta lo Luffy ni kalusthadu. Ace Blackbeard kosam vethukuthuntadu.

Crocodile Sand-Sand Fruit (Logia) user. Luffy Crocodile tho 3 rounds fight chesthadu:
1st Round: Rainbase desert lo Crocodile Luffy ni sand hook tho podichi underground sand lo paathesi complete ga defeat chesthadu.
2nd Round: Luffy sand ki water weak point ani telsukoni Water-Luffy ga fight chesthadu, kani Crocodile Luffy body lo water motham suck chesi dry mummy la marchi vadilesthadu.
3rd Round: Underground Royal Tomb lo, Luffy tana swantha blood (raktham) ni chetulaki pooskoni, Crocodile Sand body ni hit chesthadu. Gomu Gomu no Storm tho Crocodile ni ground nunchi aakasam loki break chesi defeat chesthadu!

Civil war aagipothundi, Arabasta lo varsham kurusthundi.
Straw Hats bayalderuthunte, Princess Vivi country ni rule cheyadaniki akkade undipovalsi vasthundi. Marines chusthunnaru kabatti Vivi Straw Hats tho openly matladaledu. Kani Straw Hats andharu tana venuka tirigi, tana left hands ni aakasam loki ethi tana wrist meeda unna "X" mark ni chupistharu â€” "Memu ekkadunna manam eppatiki Nakama (Friends)!"

Ship loki vellaka, Nico Robin Going Merry lo dagi untundi: "Luffy, nannu bathikinchav, so nannu nee crew lo theeskovali!" ani Robin Straw Hats Archaeologist ga join avthundi.

4. SKY ISLAND SAGA: DREAMS & THE GOLDEN BELL OF SKYPEIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[JAYA & BLACKBEARD'S PHILOSOPHY]
Aakasam nunchi oka giant ship padipothundi. Straw Hats Sky Island (Skypiea) gurinchi vethukuthu "Jaya" island ki vastharu.
Akkada Bellamy ane arrogant rookie pirate Luffy & Zoro ni "Sky island antu em ledu, dreams antha waste" ani pub lo kotti mock chesthadu. Luffy & Zoro thirigi kottaru (just like Shanks).
Pub bayataki vachaka, oka pedda manishi cherry pie thintu kurchuntadu... athane "Marshall D. Teach" (Blackbeard)!
Teach Luffy tho antadu:
"A man's dream will never die! (Hito no yume wa owaranai!) Vallu nee meeda navvuthunnara? Navvani! Prapancham lo goppa vaallu andharu ilaanti navvula madhyalone puttukocharu!"

Luffy tarvatha Bellamy ni single punch tho face smash chesi odisthadu. Mont Blanc Cricket and monkeys help tho "Knock Up Stream" (ocean vertical geyser) ekkuthu Going Merry aakasam loki egiripothundi!


[SKYPEIA & GOD ENEL]
Straw Hats 10,000 meters high aakasam lo unna "White Sea" & Skypiea reach avtharu.
Akkada "God Enel" ane powerful tyrant rule chesthuntadu. Enel daggira "Goro Goro no Mi" (Lightning Logia Fruit) and high-level Mantra (Observation Haki) untayi. Athani power 200 Million Volts lightning!

Skypiea lo 400 years flashback bayatapaduthundi: Great warrior Calgara and explorer Mont Blanc Noland friendship katha. Upper Yard anedi nijaaniki Jaya island nunchi egiripoyina oka mukkha. Akkada unna Golden Bell ni Noland malli vinapadela ring cheyali ane promise undipoyindi.

Enel island mothanni destroy chesi Moon (Fairy Vearth) meedhaki vellalani plan chesthadu. Kani Luffy body rubber kabatti Lightning Luffy ni emi cheyaledu (Natural counter!).
Luffy tana chethiki oka giant golden ball ni attach cheskoni, Enel Raigo lightning ball ni baddhalu kotti, Enel ni Golden Bell meedhaki kodathadu!
Aa Golden Bell sound aakasam nunchi kindhaki Jaya lo unna Mont Blanc Cricket ki vinipisthundi â€” "Sky Island nijamgaane undi!" ani proof dorkuthundi.

5. WATER 7 & ENIES LOBBY SAGA: NAKAMA SACRIFICE & GEAR UP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[AOKIJI ENCOUNTER & WATER 7 CRISIS]
Long Ring Long Land tarvatha, Marine Admiral "Aokiji" (Kuzan - Ice Logia) appear avthadu. Admiral power mundu Straw Hats helpless ga nilabadatharu. Robin and Luffy ni ice statues ga marchesthadu. Straw Hats ki first time World Government true military power telusthundi.

Straw Hats tana Going Merry ni repair cheyinchadaniki legendary shipwright city "Water 7" ki vastharu.
Galley-La Company master shipwrights Merry ni inspect chesi cheptharu: "Ship keel complete ga broken. Ee ship inka next island ki vellaledu. Vellina munigipothundi!"
Luffy captain ga oka painful decision theeskuntadu: Merry ni vadilesi kotha ship konali ani.
Kani Usopp Merry ni tana praanam la preminchadu (Kaya gift). Usopp Luffy tho godava padi, captaincy ni challenge chesthadu:
"Going Merry kosam nenu neetho duel chestha!"
Luffy vs Usopp heart-breaking fight jaruguthundi. Luffy Usopp ni defeat chesthadu kani edusthu Merry ni Usopp ki ichesi Straw Hats nunchi bayataki pampesthadu.


[CP9 BETRAYAL & ROBIN'S SECRET]
Ade time lo, Robin sudden ga disappear avthundi. World Government top assassination unit "CP-9" (Rob Lucci, Kaku, Blueno, Kalifa) Galley-La workers ga 5 years nunchi undercover lo untaru. Vallu Ancient Weapon "Pluton" blueprint kosam Iceburg ni target chestharu.
Robin tana Straw Hats friends ni Buster Call (island destruction military fleet) nunchi kapadadaniki, tana swantha life ni sacrifice chesi CP9 ki surrender avthundi.
CP-9 Franky (Pluton blueprint holder) and Robin ni theskoni judicial island "Enies Lobby" ki train lo theskeltharu.


[ENIES LOBBY: DECLARATION OF WAR]
Straw Hats, Franky Family, and Galley-La shipwrights kalisi Sea Train "Rocketman" meeda Enies Lobby meedhaki ride chestharu.
Robin Courthouse balcony meeda nilabadi untundi, kindha CP9 members untaru. Enies Lobby lo unna 10,000 Marines ni Luffy okkade beat chesthu courthouse roof meedhaki reach avthadu.
Straw Hats andharu (Luffy, Zoro, Sanji, Nami, Chopper, and Sogeking/Usopp in mask) roof meeda line ga nilabadatharu!

Luffy Robin ni aduguthadu: "Robin! Nuvvu inka bathakalani cheppaledhu!"
Spandam (CP9 chief) antadu: "Prapancham lo unna 170 nations unna World Government flag adi! Meeru daaniki eduruthirigithe prapanchanike shatruvulu avtharu!"
Luffy ventane Sogeking vaipu chusi single command isthadu:
"Sogeking... shoot down that flag!"
Sogeking tana slingshot tho World Government flag ni mandinchesthadu!
Luffy prapancha prabhutvaniki direct ga YUDDHAM prakatisthadu!

Luffy arusthadu: "ROBIN! SAY YOU WANT TO LIVE!"
Robin kanti nunchi tears rivers la flow avthu, tana childhood pain (Ohara destruction) marchipoyi gundela ninda arusthundi:
"IKITAI! (I WANT TO LIVE! Take me out to the sea with you!)"


[THE EPIC BATTLES & GOING MERRY FAREWELL]
Enies Lobby lo epic battles jaruguthayi:
- Luffy reveals "Gear 2nd" (blood pumping speed) and "Gear 3rd" (bone balloon giant limbs).
- Zoro reveals "Asura" (9-sword style demon form) defeating Kaku.
- Sanji reveals "Diable Jambe" (flaming leg) defeating Jabra.
- Chopper uses 3 Rumble Balls and transforms into uncontrollable "Monster Point".
- Luffy vs Rob Lucci (Leopard Zoan) ultimate brutal fight. Lucci tana Rokuogan tho Luffy ni chavu anchula daggiriki theskelthadu. Kani Usopp mask teesesi arusthadu: "Luffy! Get up! Ekkada chachipodaaniki raledhu manam!"
Luffy tana entire willpower tho Gear 2nd "Gomu Gomu no Jet Gatling" tho Lucci ni goadalo petti prapancham baddhalayye range lo kotti defeat chesthaadu!

Buster Call bombs paduthunte, Straw Hats escape avvaleni situation lo ocean nunchi oka voice vinipisthundi:
"Kindhaki dookandi! Ocean loki dookandi!"
Akkada no one other than mana "Going Merry" vachi vallani kapaduthundi! Merry tana swantha spirit (Klabautermann) tho chivari sari tana friends ni save cheyadaniki ocean ni daatukoni vachindi.

Escape ayyaka, ocean madhyalo Merry mukkhaluga virigipothundi.
Luffy Merry ki Viking funeral (agni sanskaram) chesthadu. Merry burning avthunte aakasam nunchi Merry voice vinipisthundi:
"Gomen ne... Nenu meetho inka mundhuku ralechanu. Kani nenu meetho unna prathi kshanam chaala santhosham ga unnanu. Thank you for loving me!"
Straw Hats andharu gundelu pagilela edustharu (Anime history lo ship kosam edchina oke okka moment).

Water 7 lo Franky Straw Hats kosam indestructible Adam Wood tho "Thousand Sunny" ane super ship ni build chesthadu. Franky Straw Hats Shipwright ga join avthadu. Usopp tana ego marchipoyi Luffy ki sorry cheppi malli join avthadu.
Akkade Marine Hero Vice Admiral "Monkey D. Garp" (Luffy grandfather) vasthadu and reveal chesthadu: Luffy father is Dragon, and Yonko (Four Emperors) rule the New World!

6. THRILLER BARK SAGA: GHOST ISLAND & ZORO'S GREATEST MOMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Florian Triangle ane spooky mist ocean lo Straw Hats giant floating island ship "Thriller Bark" loki enter avtharu.
Akkada "Brook" ane living skeleton musician kalusthadu (Revive-Revive Fruit / Yomi Yomi no Mi user). Brook 50 years nunchi tana solo ship lo skeletons madhyalo ontari ga brathukuthuntadu. Athani shadow ni Warlord "Gekko Moriah" (Shadow-Shadow Fruit) dongilinchadu. And Brook evaro kaadu... Reverse Mountain daggira unna whale Laboon kosam wait chesthunna Rumbar Pirates chivari member!

Moriah Luffy shadow theskoni legendary giant "Oars" body lo pedthadu.
